Live board

Stations that need help come first. "Needs jars now" means the volunteer tapped "Last jar", or the station will run dry before a runner could get there (runner trip time + 10 minutes). Run-dry time = last swap + jars left × minutes per jar. Minutes per jar is the average of the last few swaps, or the plan until a station has two swaps. "Not stocked" means the volunteer has not counted and confirmed the jars and cups at the start. "No taps" means nobody has tapped for 1.5× the minutes per jar (at least 10), so check on the volunteer.
