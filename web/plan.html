How this is worked out: people at a station × water per person per hour × weather factor. Jars = litres ÷ 20, rounded up. Cups = litres ÷ 0.2 (200 ml cup, an assumption). The 0.5 L high value follows a government advisory for outdoor summer events (500 ml per person per hour). The 0.25 L low value and the weather factors (1.3, 1.6) are our assumptions. Change them if you know better. 1 station per 500 people follows public-event guidance from Northern Territory, Australia.
